### 🔗 Email Delivery via Direct SMTP (Zoho Mail)

Emails are now sent directly from the FastAPI backend via SMTP — no n8n required.

  #### Email triggers

  1. Lead form submission (`POST /webhook`) → AI-drafted reply is emailed to the lead
     - Subject:  FantomAI | Lead Intake Proposal & Next Steps
     - Sender:    Fantom AI <team@fantomai.site>
  2. Appointment booking (`POST /api/appointments`) → confirmation email to the requester
     - Subject:  FantomAI | Genesis Session Confirmation
  3. Admin login → 2FA code email

  #### Configuration (.env / Render env vars)

    SMTP_HOST=smtp.zoho.com
    SMTP_PORT=587
    SMTP_USERNAME=team@fantomai.site
    SMTP_PASSWORD=your_app_password
    SMTP_FROM_EMAIL=team@fantomai.site
    SMTP_FROM_NAME='Fantom AI'
    SMTP_USE_TLS=true
    CALENDAR_URL=https://cal.com/moaz-kashif-ovkhut/30min   # used in AI email replies

  Note: Free-plan Zoho Mail accounts must use smtp.zoho.com (not
  smtppro.zoho.com, which is for paid plans). SMTP access must be enabled
  in the mailbox, and an app-specific password is required if the account
  has two-factor authentication enabled.
